Sponsor Steerglass

Steerglass is free and open source, with no accounts, no telemetry and nothing locked behind a plan. Sponsors keep it that way.

Steerglass is built and maintained by Davide Bettio. Sponsorship pays for the time that goes into it — new features, support for more clusters and setups, fixing what you report — and for what it costs to ship it: Apple’s developer program, the servers behind steerglass.com and its downloads, the apt and dnf repositories.

For companies

If your team operates Kubernetes with Steerglass, or you build for people who run Kubernetes every day, sponsoring puts your name in front of exactly them. Sponsors get:

1

On the website

Your logo and a link on this page and at the bottom of the steerglass.com home page.

2

In the README

Your logo and a link in the README on GitHub, the first page people see of the project.

3

In release notes

A thank-you in the notes of the releases you support, on the changelog and on GitHub.

Steerglass itself stays free of ads: sponsors are never shown inside the app. Sponsorship doesn’t buy decisions about the roadmap or the code either, but we’re always glad to hear what your team needs.

Write to hello@steerglass.com

Tell us about your company and how you’d like to help, and we’ll reply with the options. Open source programs — credits, licenses, services — are welcome too.

For individuals

If Steerglass saves you time, the best support costs nothing:

  • Star it on GitHub — it helps other people find it.
  • Tell your team, or write about it.
  • Report bugs and ideas in the issues, and send pull requests.

Recurring sponsorship through GitHub Sponsors is coming soon.
