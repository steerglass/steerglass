Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

The short version

  • Your kubeconfig, credentials and everything in your clusters stay on your computer. Steerglass talks to your clusters directly, never through us.
  • Steerglass has no accounts, no telemetry and no crash reporting. The app only contacts steerglass.com to check for and download updates.
  • The website uses cookieless, anonymous analytics (Umami, on our own server). No ads, no trackers, nothing sold or shared for marketing.
  • Steerglass is open source: you can check all of this in the code.

Who we are

Steerglass (the desktop app and steerglass.com) is made by Davide Bettio, Italy, who is the data controller for the processing described here. For anything about your data, write to hello@steerglass.com.

What we collect, and why

What the app sends

Steerglass reads your kubeconfig and connects directly from your computer to your Kubernetes clusters. Your kubeconfig, tokens and certificates, cluster names and addresses, resources, logs, secrets and terminal sessions are never sent to us. The app has no accounts, no telemetry and no crash reporting.

The app only contacts steerglass.com to check for updates (it downloads a small file with the latest version number) and to download them. Like any web request, this reaches our server logs (see below).

Website analytics

We use Umami, self-hosted on our server, to count visits and see what works on the website: pages viewed (without query strings, except campaign tags such as utm_source that tell us which link brought you), referring site, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and approximate location (country, region, city) derived from your IP address. We also count a few actions, such as which installer is downloaded.

Umami sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. Visits are grouped with an identifier computed from your IP address and browser with a salt that changes every month, so you can’t be followed over time or across sites, and we can’t tell who you are. These statistics are not shared with anyone.

Server logs and security

Like every web server, ours records technical data for each request — IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, browser user agent, and response status — to keep the service running and secure and to prevent abuse. These logs are rotated automatically and kept for a few weeks at most.

Issues and feedback on GitHub

Bug reports, ideas and feedback go to GitHub Issues, where they are public. When you open one from the app, it fills in the app version and operating system; you can edit them before posting. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement.

Emails you send us

If you write to us by email, we keep the conversation to answer you and for as long as it’s useful for support.

Accounts from the beta

During the beta, Steerglass had optional accounts. They are no longer needed — every feature and every cluster work without one — so we have deleted them, together with the computers and support requests stored for them. Any copies left in backups disappear within 6 months as old backups expire.

Legal bases

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): update checks, security logs and abuse prevention, backups, and anonymous, cookieless website analytics to improve the website. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

How long we keep it

  • Server logs: a few weeks at most. Analytics: kept as anonymous statistics.
  • Accounts from the beta: deleted; old backups that still contain them expire within 6 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access your data, get a copy in a portable format, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to hello@steerglass.com; we’ll answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or the authority where you live or work.

Children

Steerglass is a professional tool and isn’t meant for children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect their data.
