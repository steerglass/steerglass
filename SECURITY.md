# Security policy

Steerglass holds the keys to your clusters, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Report it privately instead:

- through GitHub: *Security* tab → *Report a vulnerability*, or
- by email to [hello@steerglass.com](mailto:hello@steerglass.com), with "security" in the subject.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We'll confirm we
received it within a few days, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you in the release notes if
you like.

## Supported versions

Fixes go into the latest release; builds from steerglass.com update themselves. Please check that the
problem still happens there.

## Scope

In scope: the Steerglass app, its update mechanism, and steerglass.com. Research in good faith is
welcome and we won't take action against it. Please don't access other people's data, don't degrade
the service (no load or denial-of-service testing) and don't use social engineering.

How updates are signed and verified is described on [steerglass.com/security](https://steerglass.com/security/).
